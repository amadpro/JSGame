About SPause

"Spause" is an platformer game where you use the pause and sliders to complete obstacles. Strategize your next move with pause button and sliders to complete the level. The origin of it's name is Pausing and Space, as the character is themed to be on space.

  • Pause Button as a obstacles
  • Press option button to use sliders
  • Gravity slider to adjust the gravity
  • Brightness slider to adjust the brightness
  • Move the sliders as an obstacle to complete levels
